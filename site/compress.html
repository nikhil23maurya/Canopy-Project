How do I open the file I downloaded?

Open Restore and choose the .huf file you downloaded from Canopy. You can then download the original in its original format.

Why did my file get bigger?

Photos, videos and ZIP files are often already compressed. Very small files can also grow because Canopy adds information needed to restore them. Keep the original if it’s smaller.
